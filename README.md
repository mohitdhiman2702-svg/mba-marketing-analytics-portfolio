# MBA Marketing & Analytics Portfolio

Welcome to my professional portfolio showcasing projects in marketing analytics, budget optimisation, FMCG brand strategy and competitor benchmarking.

## Projects

### 01. Marketing Budget Optimisation & ROI Simulator

An Excel-based marketing analytics project focused on evaluating campaign performance and optimising budget allocation across digital and traditional marketing channels.

**Key areas:**
- Marketing budget allocation
- Campaign performance analysis
- CTR, CPC, CPA, CVR and ROAS
- ROI evaluation
- Data-driven budget recommendations

*Note: The campaign dataset is synthetic and intended for illustrative analysis.*

### 02. MAGGI vs Sunfeast YiPPee! – Brand Growth & Competitor Benchmarking

A comparative FMCG marketing research project examining two leading instant noodle brands in India.

**Key areas:**
- Brand positioning and competitor benchmarking
- Product, pricing and promotional strategy
- STP and 4Ps analysis
- SWOT analysis
- Google Trends research
- Strategic growth recommendations

*Note: Google Trends represents relative search interest, not actual sales or market share.*
